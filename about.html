Our Story & Mission

We Are On A Mission To Automate The Impossible.

At Antic Mitra, we believe modern businesses should not be constrained by manual grunt work, missed customer calls, or clunky legacy systems. We build autonomous AI software and growth engines that give founders their time and freedom back.

Part 1: The "Antic" Spirit

Daring to Do Things Differently

The word Antic represents playful defiance of tradition, unconventional ingenuity, and the courage to question bloated orthodoxies.

While others build complex software that requires months of staff training and manuals, we build AI agents so intuitive they run smoothly in the background. We reject outdated legacy patterns in favor of what is truly efficient, fast, and scalable.

In ancient Sanskrit, Mitra translates to friend, ally, companion. We do not act as a distant vendor handing off cold code and disappearing into the night.

We are your true technical partner. We celebrate your wins, obsess over your customer satisfaction metrics, and stand shoulder-to-shoulder with you through every phase of scale.
